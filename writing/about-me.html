About Me

I build AI-native enterprise products, and I like proving the judgment behind them, not just the outcomes.

For the last two and a half years, I've been a Product Manager working at the intersection of enterprise workflows and AI agents, most recently leading an AI-native employee experience platform that scaled to over 1 million end users across 50+ Fortune 500 pilots in six months. Before that, I worked in QA at an AI startup, which is where I first got obsessed with the gap between a demo that impresses a room and a system that survives production.

That gap is what I write about here.

What I actually do

I own products end to end, from customer discovery through architecture decisions to shipping. I've built AI agents on knowledge graphs instead of vector search when the problem needed deterministic multi-hop reasoning. I've built eval frameworks that gate releases at 90%+ accuracy across multiple foundation models. I've killed features after testing showed they weren't viable, which is a decision I'd make again every time.

What you'll find here

Three things:

  • Articles on product and technical decisions I've made, written for people who want the reasoning, not just the result
  • Prototypes you can click through, because a static case study is worse than something you can actually use
  • Real code, built to learn rather than to demo, because I'd rather show you something I understand deeply than something that looks impressive from a distance

Outside of work

I cook, play badminton, and read. Cooking taught me precision and improvisation at the same time. Badminton taught me that sustainable pace beats sprinting. Reading keeps me honest about what I don't know yet. All three show up in how I build products.

I'm currently exploring my next role, focused on AI-native product work at companies building genuinely new things, not bolting AI onto old workflows.
